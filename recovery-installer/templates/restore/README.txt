RODIN Super Restore 11G v1.0

Device: POCO X7 Pro / Redmi Turbo 4 (rodin)
Purpose: restore the factory physical super geometry (11 GiB).

WARNING: this destroys the current 20 GiB super contents and userdata.

After SUCCESS:
1. Reboot directly back to recovery.
2. Format Data.
3. Reboot recovery once.
4. Flash an 11 GiB-compatible super.img.

Do not boot Android before formatting data and flashing a valid super image.
